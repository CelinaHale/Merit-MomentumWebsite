Our recruiting model

Your employees. Your payroll. Our recruiting support.

Merit & Momentum operates through an employer paid direct hire recruiting model. We help identify interested candidates for positions that employers intend to fill directly.

The employer conducts its own interviews, makes the final hiring decision, and handles wages, payroll taxes, benefits, onboarding, and other employment responsibilities.

We charge an agreed placement fee when a candidate we've referred successfully begins employment, according to the written recruiting agreement. Candidates are never charged for employment opportunities through Merit & Momentum.

Understanding the difference

Direct hire recruiting works differently from temporary staffing.

Temporary staffing companies generally employ workers during their assignments and charge employers an hourly rate covering wages, employment costs, administration, and the staffing company's margin.

Merit & Momentum helps employers find people they can hire directly. Our service uses an agreed placement fee rather than ongoing hourly staffing charges.

Feature Temporary Staffing Merit & Momentum
Worker employed by Generally the agency Employer directly
Worker wages paid by Agency Employer
Recruiting charges Usually included in ongoing hourly billing One agreed placement fee
Typical purpose Temporary or flexible staffing Permanent or ongoing positions

Some staffing agencies also offer direct hire recruiting. Actual costs, services, and employment responsibilities depend on each provider's agreement.

How it works

A straightforward process from inquiry to hire.

01

Tell us what you need.

Complete our employer inquiry with information about your organization, open positions, required qualifications, and preferred hiring timeline.

02

Confirm the agreement.

We discuss the role, confirm the applicable placement fee, and establish recruiting responsibilities and payment terms in writing before beginning the search.

03

Review potential candidates.

We identify and communicate with interested candidates, review relevant experience, and present suitable candidates for your consideration with their permission.

04

Make the hiring decision.

Your business conducts interviews, makes its final employment decision, extends job offers, and handles onboarding and employer required screening.

05

Complete the placement.

Once a referred candidate starts employment, the agreed placement fee is earned. We invoice the employer, and payment is due within seven calendar days of the invoice date under our standard terms.

Payment and expectations

Clear terms help everyone make informed decisions.

There is no upfront fee simply to submit a hiring inquiry. Before recruiting begins, we confirm the position, service scope, placement fee, and payment conditions in a written agreement.

Employers pay for successful direct hire placements. The placement fee is earned on the employee's first day of work, and payment is due within seven calendar days of the invoice date under our standard terms.

Employers retain responsibility for hiring decisions, workplace supervision, wages, payroll, and applicable employment obligations. Candidates never pay for placement services.

No recruiting search guarantees a successful hire. Any replacement search arrangements or additional services must be expressly included in the written agreement.
