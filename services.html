Our recruiting model

Your employees. Your payroll. Our recruiting support.

Merit & Momentum operates through an employer paid, direct hire recruiting model. We work to identify prospective candidates for positions an employer intends to fill directly.

The employer conducts its own hiring decisions, extends employment offers, and pays wages, payroll taxes, benefits, and other employment costs associated with its workforce.

We charge an agreed placement fee when a referred candidate successfully begins employment, as outlined in our written recruiting agreement. Candidates are never charged for employment opportunities through Merit & Momentum.

Understanding the difference

Direct hire recruiting works differently from temporary staffing.

Temporary staffing companies generally employ workers during an assignment and charge the business an hourly bill rate that helps cover wages, employment costs, administrative services, and the agency's margin.

Merit & Momentum does not employ or supply temporary workers under this service. We help employers find candidates they can hire directly, with an agreed placement fee rather than ongoing staffing agency charges.

Feature Temporary Staffing Merit & Momentum
Employee hired by Usually staffing agency Employer directly
Worker wages paid by Staffing agency Employer
Agency billing Typically ongoing hourly rate One time placement fee
Common use Temporary and flexible labor needs Permanent and ongoing openings

Some staffing agencies also provide direct hire services. Actual costs and responsibilities depend on the provider and agreement.

How it works

A straightforward process from inquiry to hire.

01

Tell us what you need.

Complete our employer inquiry with information about your organization, open positions, qualifications, and hiring timeline.

02

Confirm the assignment.

We discuss the position requirements, determine the applicable pricing classification, and establish the recruiting scope and written payment terms before beginning work.

03

Review potential candidates.

We identify and communicate with prospective candidates, consider relevant qualifications, and present interested candidates with their permission for your review.

04

Make the hiring decision.

Your business handles its interviews, final evaluations, hiring decision, and employment offer. Any employer required checks or onboarding steps remain your responsibility unless separately agreed.

05

Complete the placement.

When an eligible referred candidate begins employment, the agreed placement fee becomes payable according to the written recruiting agreement. No ongoing staffing markup applies.

Payment & expectations

Clear terms help everyone make informed decisions.

There is no upfront fee simply to submit a hiring inquiry. Before a recruiting search begins, we confirm the role, service scope, fee, and payment conditions in writing.

Under our standard direct hire model, the employer pays the agreed placement fee following a successful hire, according to the agreement. Employers remain responsible for their own hiring and employment obligations.

We do not guarantee that every search will result in a successful placement. Any replacement arrangements, candidate screening requirements, or additional services must be expressly included in the written agreement.
