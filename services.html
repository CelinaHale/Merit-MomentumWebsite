Our business model

Direct hire recruiting with clear responsibilities.

Merit & Momentum Recruiting helps employers identify interested candidates for positions they intend to fill directly. The employer interviews and hires the worker, handles payroll and employment obligations, and makes all final employment decisions.

Rather than billing for hours worked through a temporary staffing arrangement, we charge one agreed placement fee when a referred candidate begins employment.

Our additional guidance helps employers locate relevant bonding and workforce resources when those programs may support a particular hire. Candidates never pay a recruiting fee.

Included recruiting guidance

Hiring support can include more than finding a candidate.

Some employers are unfamiliar with programs that may help reduce specific risks or support workforce development. As part of our recruiting service, Merit & Momentum can help identify relevant programs and guide employers toward the agencies responsible for them.

01

Federal bonding program navigation

We help employers understand the Federal Bonding Program and connect with Arkansas program contacts. Eligible hires may receive no cost fidelity bond coverage, commonly $5,000 for six months, against covered employee dishonesty losses. A job offer with a start date is generally needed before issuance. The administering program determines eligibility and coverage.

02

Training and workforce programs

We help identify Arkansas Workforce Services and local workforce development resources that may support eligible employers with recruiting, training, or retaining workers. Any funding, reimbursement, or participation is subject to program availability and approval.

03

Current tax incentive resources

We direct employers to current federal and state information and appropriate tax professionals. The Work Opportunity Tax Credit is not currently available for employees beginning work after December 31, 2025. We will not represent expired incentives as guaranteed savings.

04

Screening and hiring considerations

We support informed hiring conversations that consider job requirements, qualifications, relevant background concerns, and applicable restrictions. Employers conduct their own screening and remain responsible for legal compliance and final hiring decisions.

05

Practical onboarding and retention guidance

We can help employers identify useful workforce contacts and discuss clear expectations, appropriate training, and workplace practices that support successful employment.

Clear expectations

Our responsibilities and yours.

Merit & Momentum provides direct hire recruiting and relevant program navigation. The employer retains responsibility for all employment decisions, compensation, screening, payroll, supervision, and compliance obligations.

Placement fees and any agreed replacement search terms are confirmed in writing. Our standard payment term is seven calendar days from the invoice date after the referred employee begins work.

Programs such as federal bonding and workforce training are administered independently. Eligibility, funding, insurance coverage, and approvals are determined by their respective agencies or providers.

Our guidance does not replace legal, tax, insurance, or professional compliance advice. We cannot guarantee that an employer will qualify for an incentive or that recruiting will result in a successful hire.
