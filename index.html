What we do

Better hiring begins with seeing the whole picture.

Merit & Momentum Recruiting works with employers who want dependable people and a hiring process grounded in what a position actually requires. We look beyond narrow assumptions and consider the experience, work ethic, reliability, readiness, and potential a candidate brings to the workplace.

That approach gives employers access to talent they may otherwise overlook while giving qualified candidates a more meaningful opportunity to demonstrate what they can contribute.

For employers

Hiring should begin with what the work actually requires.

We take the time to understand the role, the environment, and the qualities that make someone successful within your organization before identifying candidates who may be a strong fit.

The result is a more intentional recruiting process designed around the needs of the business and the people capable of meeting them.

For candidates

A resume should not be the only part of your story an employer sees.

Merit & Momentum helps candidates present a more complete picture of their experience, strengths, readiness, and professional goals. We want employers to understand what a person can contribute today, not simply the circumstances that may have shaped yesterday.

We are building relationships with employers who are willing to evaluate people thoughtfully and who understand that strong workers do not always arrive through traditional hiring pipelines.

Our approach

Good recruiting requires enough attention to understand both sides of the employment relationship.

01

We begin with the realities of the workplace.

Understanding the position, expectations, environment, and employer comes before presenting a candidate.

02

Candidates are considered in context.

Skills, experience, reliability, growth, readiness, and potential are evaluated together rather than treating one detail as the entirety of a person.

03

The connection should make sense after the hire.

Filling a vacancy is only useful when the placement has a genuine chance to work for both the employer and the person accepting it.
