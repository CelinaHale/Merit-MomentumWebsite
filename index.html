Why we exist

Hiring works better when people have a fair opportunity to show what they can do.

A resume can tell you where someone has worked, but it doesn't always capture their experience, skills, or readiness for a new opportunity. We want employers to make informed decisions and candidates to have a meaningful way to introduce themselves, including people with employment gaps or criminal records.

How our service works

Employers hire directly, and we help find the people.

Merit & Momentum is a direct hire recruiting business. We help employers identify interested candidates, but the employer makes the hiring decision and handles the employee's wages, benefits, and payroll.

Instead of an ongoing temporary staffing markup, employers pay one agreed placement fee when a candidate we referred begins working. Job seekers never pay us for employment opportunities.
