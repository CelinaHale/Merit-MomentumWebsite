Merit & Momentum Recruiting welcomes people with different work histories and experiences. Whether you're changing careers, returning to work, or looking for an opportunity after setbacks, we'd like to learn about your skills and employment interests.

A place to begin

Your experience deserves to be considered.

Not everyone has a traditional resume or a continuous employment history. That shouldn't stop someone from taking the first step toward finding work.

We welcome candidates who have relevant experience, those learning new skills, and people seeking second chance employment. We want to understand what you're prepared to contribute and what type of opportunity would be right for you.

You don't need to submit a resume to introduce yourself. The information you share in the form below helps us begin understanding your qualifications and goals.
