Privacy Policy

We recognize that professional opportunities depend on trust. This policy explains how Merit & Momentum Recruiting collects, uses, and handles information submitted through our website.

Effective November 3, 2026

1. Information we collect

When employers or candidates complete our website forms, we collect the information they choose to provide.

Employer inquiries may include business names, contact details, hiring locations, available positions, staffing needs, and relevant comments.

Candidate introductions may include names, contact details, employment interests, work experience, qualifications, education, availability, resume links, and career goals.

Our website is not intended to collect Social Security numbers, financial account information, medical records, or criminal history details through these initial inquiry forms.

2. How we use your information

Information provided through our forms may be used to:

  • Respond to employer hiring inquiries.
  • Understand candidates' skills and career interests.
  • Communicate about potentially relevant opportunities.
  • Evaluate possible recruiting relationships.
  • Maintain appropriate business records.
  • Protect our website and prevent misuse.

Submitting information does not guarantee a job offer, placement, recruiting agreement, or business relationship.

3. Sharing information

We do not sell candidate or employer information.

Candidate information will not be sent to prospective employers without the candidate's permission. We may use service providers to receive, store, and communicate about submissions.

Information may also be disclosed when legally required or when necessary to protect rights, security, or the integrity of our services.

4. Website forms and service providers

Our website is hosted through GitHub Pages. Employer and candidate forms are processed using Formspree, an external form submission service.

These providers may process technical information needed to deliver their services, maintain security, and operate their systems.

You can review the Formspree Privacy Policy and GitHub Privacy Statement .

5. Information retention

We intend to retain information only as long as reasonably necessary for recruiting, communication, legitimate business records, or applicable legal obligations.

Retention may depend on whether a candidate remains interested in opportunities, whether an employer inquiry develops into a business relationship, and whether records must be maintained for legal purposes. Our service providers may have their own retention practices.

6. Security

We aim to handle information responsibly and limit access to people who need it for legitimate business purposes. Information transmitted over the internet or stored electronically cannot be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Please avoid submitting sensitive personal information through our introductory forms.

7. Access, correction, and deletion requests

You may request information about the personal information you submitted, ask that inaccurate information be corrected, or request deletion, subject to applicable legal and recordkeeping obligations.

Until a dedicated privacy contact channel is established, candidates may submit a request through our candidate contact form and employers through our employer inquiry form. Indicate that your message concerns a privacy request in the additional information field. Do not submit sensitive identity documents through these forms.

We may need to verify a requester's identity before providing, correcting, or deleting information.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as our recruiting operations, website services, or information handling practices change. Any revised version will be published here with an updated effective date.
