Privacy Policy

We understand that sharing personal or business information requires trust. This policy explains what information Merit & Momentum Recruiting collects through our website, how we intend to use it, and how you can contact us with questions about your information.

1. Information we collect

Merit & Momentum Recruiting collects information that visitors voluntarily provide when using our website forms or contacting us directly.

Candidate information may include your name, email address, phone number, general location, employment interests, availability, work experience, skills, education, training, and other information you choose to provide.

Employer information may include company name, contact person's name, business email, phone number, hiring location, positions being filled, number of openings, hiring timeline, and information about workforce needs.

General inquiries may include your name, email address, and the contents of your message.

The website's hosting provider and other third party services may also process technical information, such as IP addresses, browser details, and website request information, as part of delivering their services.

2. How we use information

Information submitted through our website is used for purposes related to recruiting, hiring inquiries, and communication.

  • Responding to questions and service inquiries.
  • Understanding an employer's hiring needs.
  • Reviewing candidate qualifications, employment interests, and availability.
  • Contacting candidates about potentially relevant employment opportunities.
  • Communicating with employers about recruiting services and potential matches.
  • Maintaining business records and responding to appropriate requests or legal obligations.

Submitting a candidate introduction does not guarantee employment, an interview, or a referral to an employer. Submitting an employer inquiry does not create a recruiting agreement.

3. When information may be shared

Candidate information is collected to understand qualifications and identify potentially suitable job opportunities. We intend to obtain a candidate's permission before sharing their application details with a prospective employer.

Employer information may be used to evaluate and discuss recruiting needs and to communicate with prospective candidates about relevant opportunities.

Information may also be processed by service providers supporting our website, forms, communications, or business operations. We may disclose information when required by applicable law or a valid legal process.

We do not operate a business that sells candidate applications or employer contact information as marketing lists.

4. Third party services

Our website uses services provided by other companies. Those providers may process information according to their own privacy policies.

GitHub Pages: Our website is hosted through GitHub Pages. GitHub may process technical information associated with requests to access our website.

Formspree: We use Formspree to receive employer inquiries, candidate introductions, and general contact messages. When you submit a form, the information is transmitted to Formspree for processing and delivery.

Google Fonts and image services: Our website uses externally hosted fonts and photographs. Your browser may connect directly to those services when loading website content.

You can review their policies using the links below.

  • GitHub Privacy Statement
  • Formspree Privacy Policy
  • Google Privacy Policy
  • Unsplash Privacy Policy

5. Information security

We recognize the importance of protecting the information people share with us. Our website uses HTTPS, and our forms are submitted through Formspree.

No internet transmission, online service, or information storage method can guarantee complete security. Please use care when deciding what information to provide.

We ask visitors not to submit Social Security numbers, bank account details, medical records, criminal history documentation, or other highly sensitive personal information through our website forms.

6. Information retention and privacy requests

Information may be retained for as long as reasonably needed to respond to inquiries, consider candidates for employment opportunities, maintain business records, or meet applicable legal obligations.

If you have submitted information and would like to request access, correction, or deletion, contact us at meritandmomentum@yahoo.com .

Please identify the nature of your request and the email address used when submitting your information. We may need to verify that the request relates to your information before taking action.

Some information may need to be retained when required by law, necessary for legitimate recordkeeping, or subject to other applicable obligations.

7. Children's privacy

Our website is intended for people seeking employment and organizations seeking recruiting services.

We do not knowingly solicit personal information from children under the age of 13 through our website. If you believe a child has submitted information, please contact us so the situation can be reviewed.

8. Changes to this policy

Merit & Momentum Recruiting may update this Privacy Policy as our services, operating practices, or legal obligations change.

Revisions will be published on this page with an updated effective date when appropriate. We encourage visitors to review this page periodically.
