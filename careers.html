A better starting point

This page is here to help people move forward.

We know that finding work can feel frustrating, especially when you have been overlooked, judged too quickly, or told no more times than you can count. Merit & Momentum was created to make that process more human and more honest.

We work with employers who need dependable people, and we want candidates to feel comfortable introducing themselves without pretending their path has been perfect. We care about readiness, reliability, and the ability to contribute.

Who we welcome

There is more than one path to being ready for work.

You do not need to fit one narrow mold to connect with us. If you are serious about working, growing, and showing what you can bring to a workplace, we want to hear from you.

People with work experience

If you already have experience in warehouse, production, customer service, administration, trades, or other fields, we want to learn where you have worked and what you do well.

People starting over

If you have a gap in your employment history, are rebuilding after personal challenges, or are returning to the workforce, that does not automatically disqualify you from a meaningful opportunity.

People seeking a real chance

If you have a criminal record or have struggled to get employers to look past the surface, Merit & Momentum is intended to be a place where your qualifications can still be part of the conversation.

Second chance employment

Being counted out should not be the end of the story.

Second chance employment matters because many capable people never get a fair opportunity to show employers what they can do. A record, an employment gap, or a rough chapter should not erase skill, growth, or work ethic.

We are building a candidate network that gives employers a clearer picture of the person in front of them while helping job seekers present themselves with dignity and honesty.

How it works

What to expect when you reach out.

Tell us about yourself.

Complete the candidate introduction form and share your experience, strengths, location, and the types of work you are interested in.

We review your information.

We look at your work history, skills, goals, and overall readiness so we can better understand the kinds of opportunities that may fit.

We reach out when there is a fit.

As opportunities become available, we may contact you if your background aligns with what an employer is looking for.

The employer makes the final decision.

Employers handle their own interviews, hiring decisions, and onboarding. Merit & Momentum helps create the connection.
