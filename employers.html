Employer protections and incentives

More resources may be available than you realize.

When an employer considers a candidate who has faced barriers to employment, questions about screening, financial risk, and available support are reasonable. We want businesses to have useful information before making their decisions.

Merit & Momentum can help employers identify relevant programs, locate official contacts, and understand the steps involved in requesting available assistance.

01

Federal bonding protection

The Federal Bonding Program offers no cost fidelity bonds for certain workers who face employment barriers. Standard coverage provides $5,000 against specified employee dishonesty losses during the first six months of employment. We can help employers understand the program and contact the Arkansas bonding coordinator. Bonding is subject to eligibility and issuance and does not cover injuries, accidents, poor workmanship, or general employer liability.

02

Workforce training resources

Arkansas workforce programs may offer eligible employers assistance with training, developing, and retaining workers. We can help identify the appropriate local workforce center or program contact and explain what information the employer may need to provide.

03

Tax incentive information

Some workforce incentives depend on federal law, hiring dates, and eligibility requirements. The federal Work Opportunity Tax Credit is not currently available for workers who begin employment after December 31, 2025, unless federal authorization changes. We can direct employers to current agency guidance and qualified tax professionals to review any benefits that may apply.

04

Background screening practices

We can encourage employers to evaluate candidates in relation to the duties and requirements of the job, clarify screening expectations, and consult appropriate professionals about legal restrictions and compliance obligations. Background checks and final decisions remain the employer's responsibility.

05

Hiring and retention planning

We can help employers think through clear job descriptions, realistic qualifications, onboarding expectations, and access to workforce resources that may support successful employment.

Program availability and eligibility can change. Merit & Momentum does not issue bonds, grant tax credits, approve funding, or provide legal, insurance, or tax advice. Decisions and approvals are made by the responsible providers and agencies.
