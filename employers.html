How we work

Find the right people without an ongoing staffing markup.

Merit & Momentum Recruiting helps Arkansas businesses find candidates for positions they intend to fill directly. Your company hires the employee, manages payroll, and makes all final employment decisions.

We charge one agreed placement fee when a candidate we've referred begins working. Our standard fees range from $1,000 to $2,000 depending on the type of position, with custom quotes available for specialized and volume recruiting.

There's no fee simply to ask about our services. We confirm the applicable recruiting fee and service terms in writing before beginning a search.
