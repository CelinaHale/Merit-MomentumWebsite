About Merit & Momentum

We believe good recruiting starts with better judgment.

Merit & Momentum Recruiting is being built around a straightforward idea: employers deserve to understand the talent available to them, and candidates deserve to have their qualifications meaningfully considered.

Why we're here

The way we evaluate people shapes the opportunities we create.

Hiring decisions carry real consequences. For employers, a poor match affects operations, productivity, and the people already on the team. For candidates, being overlooked can mean losing an opportunity to contribute, advance, and build greater stability.

Traditional recruiting methods can be useful, but they don't always provide a complete picture. A job title, employment gap, or conventional resume doesn't necessarily reveal a person's capabilities, readiness, or ability to succeed in a particular workplace.

Merit & Momentum Recruiting is developing a more thoughtful approach to connecting people and businesses. We believe recruiters should understand what the work demands, what a candidate can contribute, and where those expectations genuinely align.

Our philosophy

Potential deserves consideration. Performance still matters.

An effective hiring process should be fair, practical, and grounded in the requirements of the position. Giving someone meaningful consideration does not mean overlooking qualifications or legitimate workplace concerns.

It means asking better questions, recognizing transferable experience, and making decisions based on relevant information rather than assumptions alone.

We believe employers and candidates both benefit when expectations are clear, ability is examined carefully, and decisions are made with professional judgment.

How we intend to work

Thoughtful recruiting requires substance behind the conversation.

Understand the workplace.

Before considering a candidate, we want to understand the employer's actual needs, responsibilities, working conditions, and expectations. A useful recommendation starts with knowing what successful performance looks like in that environment.

Recognize relevant ability.

Experience takes many forms. We intend to consider practical skills, previous responsibilities, training, communication, and transferable strengths alongside the qualifications a position requires.

Respect informed decisions.

Employers retain responsibility for their hiring decisions, and candidates deserve clear expectations about the opportunities presented to them. Our approach is built around honest communication and appropriate consideration of both parties' needs.

Build professional relationships.

Recruiting should be more than collecting applications. Our goal is to develop relationships through responsiveness, professional conduct, transparency, and a realistic understanding of what each opportunity requires.
